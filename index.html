Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Summary: Purple Rush: Arrow Maze Puzzle is built with user privacy as our top priority. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, location, or sensitive device data.

1. Information We Collect

Purple Rush operates primarily offline as an offline single-player puzzle game. We do not require account registration, email sign-ups, or social logins to play.

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or precise GPS location.
  • Local Device Storage: Game progression, high scores, unlocked levels, and sound/haptic preferences are stored locally on your device using on-device AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.

2. Permissions Used

The application only requests standard Android operating system permissions essential for core gameplay functions:

  • VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback when solving puzzles or making moves.
  • INTERNET: Used for standard network stack initialization without personal tracking.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The initial release of Purple Rush does not include third-party advertising tracking SDKs or user tracking frameworks.

4. Children’s Privacy

Purple Rush does not address anyone under the age of 13 intentionally. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Security

Because no personal data is transmitted over the internet or stored on external cloud databases, your personal data remains 100% secure on your local device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.
